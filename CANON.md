# Flow Tile canon

These are the instructions Dan has given for this project, kept in one place. They are settled. **Don't change, undo or "improve" any of them on your own**: if something here seems wrong, or a task would break it, ask Dan first. Add each new instruction here as Dan gives it, and mark one that a later instruction replaces as superseded instead of deleting it.

## How to work

1. **Source of work:** Linear, workspace *loudega*, initiative **Flow Tile**, project **Initial Prototype**. Read the initiative description first. It holds the game rules and the working rules.
2. **Order:** take the highest-priority unblocked issue, one at a time, strictly in priority order.
3. **Per issue:** follow these steps.
   - Move it to In Progress.
   - Read the issue, its Agent notes and any attached documents.
   - Do the work, commit with the issue ID in the message, and push.
   - Check every "Done when" item.

6. **Ask, don't guess.** If anything needs Dan's decision, ask Dan. Examples are making the repo public, gh auth, hosting, or a rule the issue leaves open.
7. **Quick questions first:** when Dan says to ask questions before going to bed, ask them all at once at the start, then work without stopping.
8. **Linear tracking:** make one issue per piece of new work.
   - **Issue limit** (Dan, Nov 11: "Keep using spec issues"): the workspace is at Linear's free-plan issue limit, so don't create new issues. Track each piece of work on the spec or Idea issue it builds (comment, link, status) instead.

## Words

- **Flow** is income, the water you get each turn.
- **Water** is what you have right now.
- **Gallons (gal)** measure water.
- **Storage** is how much water you can hold.
- **Mountain:** call them mountains, not boulders, in the text.
- **One set of words** (LOU-292, Dan approved Nov 12):
  - **Source** is where your river starts. **Spring** is a tile that adds +1 flow. Never call the source a spring.
  - **River** and **river's end.** Never "stream".
  - **Next water cycle** is written the same way everywhere, with a capital N only.
  - Older entries below keep their original wording where they quote Dan; the game text follows this list.

## Game rules Dan has set

- **Map 1** is flat plains, and the stream goes in one direction, **left to right**. Maps run left to right in general.
- **Tile cost:** adding a tile costs 1 water (gal).

- **Map 3** is flat with a **lake**.
- **Map 1a** introduces **sand**. ~~Sand costs 1 to enter~~ *(superseded Nov 10: sand is free to enter)* but takes away 1 flow. On the desert map with storage (5), sand also turns the bare ground next to it into grass.
- **Bare ground:** a stream through it costs 1 gal.
  - ~~**Grass:** 1 gal turns bare ground next to the river into grass, for +1 storage.~~ *Superseded Nov 12 by the growth rebalance below.*
  - ~~**Trees:** 1 more gal turns grass into trees, for +1 more storage.~~ *Superseded Nov 10 by the meadow chain below.*
  - ~~**Grass → meadow → trees** (Dan, Nov 10, LOU-261, on every map): 1 gal turns grass into a meadow, then 1 more gal turns the meadow into trees, +1 storage each step, so one tile stores up to 3 extra gal. A grown meadow counts as a meadow for pollinators.~~ *Superseded Nov 12 by the growth rebalance below.*
  - **Growth rebalance** (Dan, LOU-279, built Nov 12): storage comes from meadows, flow from trees, and grass is the free ground you build on.
    - **Grass:** free (0 gal), only on bare ground next to your river; it stores nothing. Cutting grass back to bare ground is free.
    - **Meadow** (grass → meadow, 1 gal): no storage of its own. If it touches a river tile it stores **+1 for each tree touching it** (0 to 5; wild forest counts); away from the river it stores 0. Meadows add no flow (pollinators removed).
    - **Trees** (meadow → trees, 1 gal): no storage. A grove (touching trees, all beside the river) gives **+1 flow per full 3 trees** (`floor(trees ÷ 3)`).
    - Turning a meadow into trees loses its storage but adds +1 to each meadow beside the new tree. Cutting trees back to a meadow gets the meadow's storage back.
    - Tile texts: Grass "Free next to your river. The ground you grow on." Meadow "Beside your river: +1 storage for each tree next to it." Trees "No storage. Every 3 touching trees beside your river: +1 flow."
  - Start at **3 flow and ~~3~~ 5 storage** (LOU-279 raised storage from 3 to 5 on every map with storage).
  - A river can flow through grass or trees, but washes them away.
  - Water over storage **spills** at the end of the turn.

The main choice (the next growth step) is mint-teal; the others are white. Can't afford: grey and faded with the cost still shown, and tapping it says "Need N more gal".
