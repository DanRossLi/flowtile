How to play

  • Tap the spring on the left. It makes 2 flow a turn.
  • Tap a glowing tile to extend the stream. Every tile costs 1 flow. Out of flow? End turn to collect more.
  • The land slopes down from left to right. Run the stream left to right to the sea.
  • Every level downhill adds +1 speed. Each flat (sideways) tile takes 1 speed away.
  • Reach the sea. Bloom goal: 0 tiles. Turn goal: arrive by turn 4.
